How It Works 🎨

Little Heartworks — a kids art marketplace

🛒 Requesting Art

Browse all the artwork and click "Request This!" on anything you like. Your parent will see your request and can approve it. You have a daily limit on how many pieces you can request.

✏️ Submitting Your Art

Click "Submit Art" at the top to upload a photo of your artwork, give it a title and price, and send it to your parent for approval. Once approved it appears in the shop!

🔍 Finding Art

Use the search bar to search by title or description. The more art you look at, the smarter the recommendations get!
