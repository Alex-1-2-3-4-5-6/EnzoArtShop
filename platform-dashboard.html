How It Works 🎨

Little Heartworks — a kids art marketplace

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents

Create a free account, add your kids by name, and each kid gets a unique 10-digit code. You can submit artwork on their behalf, set daily request limits, and approve or reject anything before it goes live in the shop.

🧒 Kids

Ask your parent for your 10-digit code, then click "I'm a Kid" on the home page. You can browse all the art in the shop and request pieces you like — your parent will see the requests. You can also submit your own artwork for your parent to approve!

🛍️ Buying Art

See something you love? Click "Preview & Buy", sign in with your parent account, and send a purchase request. Payment and delivery are arranged in person — we'll send an email to the seller to get things sorted.

🔍 Finding Art

Use the search bar to find art by title, description, kid name, or parent. Smart search learns what you like and shows recommendations based on art you've viewed before.
