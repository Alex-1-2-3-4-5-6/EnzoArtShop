❓ Help & Info

Little Heartworks — a kids art marketplace

👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents

Create a free account, add your kids by name, and each gets a unique 10-digit code. You approve every artwork before it goes live, set daily request limits, and handle all purchases from your inbox.

🧒 For Kids

Ask your parent for your 10-digit code, then tap "I'm a Kid" on the home page. Enter your code and name to browse the shop, request art you like (your parent sees it in their inbox), and submit your own artwork for approval!

🛍️ Buying Art

Click any artwork to open it. Fill in your email and an optional tip, then hit "Send Request to Seller". The seller gets notified and will reach out to arrange payment and pickup in person. Once both sides confirm, the art is marked sold and disappears from the shop.

🔄 Trading Art

Click "Trading" at the top to see art listed for trade. Open a piece you want and pick one of your own artworks to offer as a swap — no money involved! Accept or decline trades from your inbox.

🔍 Search & Filter

Type anything in the search bar — smart search understands what you mean even with rough descriptions. Use the All / Drawing / Painting / Digital / Other buttons to filter by type. Recommendations at the top are based on art you've looked at before.

📬 Inbox

The Inbox button (with a red dot when something's new) shows all your notifications: kid art buy requests, purchase requests from buyers, trade offers, and art waiting for your approval. Dismiss any notification with the ✕ button.
