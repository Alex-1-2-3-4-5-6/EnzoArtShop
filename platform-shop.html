❓ Help & Info

Little Heartworks — a kids art marketplace

👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents

Sign up for a free account and add your kids by name — each kid gets a unique 10-digit code. You control everything: approve or reject artwork before it goes live, set daily request limits for kids, and manage your posted art from your dashboard.

🧒 For Kids

Get your 10-digit code from your parent and click "I'm a Kid" on the home page. Browse art, request pieces you love, and submit your own drawings for your parent to approve and post to the shop!

🛍️ Buying Art

Click "Preview & Buy" on any artwork, fill in your name and email, and send a purchase request. The seller will then send you the art in person — both of you confirm when it's sent and received, and it goes to your purchase history.

🔄 Trading Art

Switch to the Trading tab to find art listed for trade. Pick one of your own artworks to offer as a swap — no money involved! The owner can accept or reject your offer from their Art Requests page.

🔍 Search & Recommendations

Search by artwork title, description, kid name, or parent name. The smart search learns what you like and shows personalized recommendations based on art you've viewed.
