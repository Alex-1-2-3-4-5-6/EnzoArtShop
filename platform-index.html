How Little Heartworks Works 🎨

A safe art marketplace made for kids and their families

👨‍👩‍👧 Parents — Create an Account

Sign up with your email address (you must be 18+). Once logged in, go to your Dashboard to add your child by name. Each child gets a unique 10-digit code — this is how they log in from the home page. You can also set a password for extra security so only your child can use their code.

🧒 Kids — Browse & Submit Art

Kids tap "I'm a Kid" on the home page and enter their 10-digit code (and password if one is set). They can then browse all the artwork in the shop, search by name or description, and request pieces they want — their parent sees the request and can approve or decline it. Kids can also submit their own artwork from the shop page, and it goes to their parent for approval before appearing in the shop.

✏️ Submitting Artwork

Parents can submit art from their Dashboard — either on behalf of a child (it waits for parent approval) or as themselves (goes straight to the shop). Kids can submit their own art using the button in their shop view. Every submission goes through a content filter and parent approval before it's visible to the public.

🛍️ Buying Art

Click any artwork to open its full page. Sign in with your parent account, fill in your name and email, and optionally add a donation on top of the price. Hit "Send Purchase Request" — the seller will be notified by email to arrange payment and delivery in person. No money is collected through the website.

🔍 Search & Recommendations

Use the search bar in the shop to find art by title, description, kid name, or parent name. The shop uses AI-powered search to understand what you mean even if you don't use exact words. The Recommended for You section learns from artworks you've clicked on and suggests similar ones — recommendations are saved to your account so they stay consistent across devices.
